Gabi V

Co-founder, ethical hacker

Gabi has spent her career getting into places she wasn't supposed to be — buildings, inboxes, phone systems — so that the people who work there don't have to learn the hard way.

She founded Aerials Security to put that same tradecraft to work on the other side of the table.

Aerials Security was co-founded by ethical hackers who got tired of watching companies pour their security budget into tools while the actual break-in happened over the phone.

So we built the company around the part of security most vendors treat as an afterthought: the people. Not with compliance videos nobody finishes, but with live simulations, real pretexts, and training built to be remembered rather than clicked through.

Every engagement — training, testing, or a keynote in front of a thousand people — is run by people who've done this work in the field, not read about it in a slide deck.
